1 · Period rate trend (hire vs CP date)

Why: the single most useful chart in this report — what the pmx period market actually paid over time. Each dot = a fixed-rate fixture at its CP date. The trend is unmistakable: ~$10–15k through 2025, ~$15–18k in Q1 2026, and $19–21k by May 2026 (MSXT HELEN $21k, REGINA $20.5k, ANDROMACHE $20k). Quote period today off the right-hand cluster, not off 2025 memories. Short periods (≤5 mos) command a premium — check the dot's tooltip before benchmarking.

2 · Tonnage coming open, month by month

Why: the re-delivery pipeline of this period fleet, stacked by redelivery area. June–September 2026 is crowded (incl. windows already open: ISMENE, MSXT BELLONA, AGIA YPOMONI…) — mostly into the Pacific, which caps Far-East spot sentiment; the Q1 2027 wave is where the 2026 spring fixtures start coming back.

3 · Days until earliest redelivery — next 15

Why: the working queue. Red = the minimum period is already served, the ship can be redelivered any day (effectively spot/relet candidates); anything under ~90 days is current re-chartering business. Useful both to chase the next employment and to spot prompt relet candidates among charterers (Element, Mizzen, CRC…).

4 · Charterer activity

Why: who has been taking pmx period. Refined Success (4), CRC (4 incl. Diana relets), Classic (4+) and the trader names (Element, Mercuria, Swissmarine) dominate — these are the natural counterparties to call with period candidates, and the red line shows what each has been paying on average (fixed-rate fixtures only).

5 · Period length taken

Why: what duration the market is actually trading. The bulk of fixtures sit in the 9–12 month bucket (one-year period remains the standard), short 3–6 month cover picked up in spring 2026 at premium rates, and only outliers go beyond 14 months (RUBINA 19–21, ARWEN GEMINI 30–36). Helps frame realistic period proposals.

6 · Broker league table

Why: who is doing the period business (owners' side; joint fixtures credited to each office). Useful for knowing who to watch — and where the competition sits on each account.

7 · Rate vs vessel age

Why: the age discount, quantified on real fixtures. Modern eco units (2022+ : SDTR, TRANSCENDEN, REGINA, MSXT HELEN) earn a clear premium; the pre-2008 ships (MU XI 2000, FORTUNE UNION 1998, EKOUO 2002) trade $4–7k below contemporaneous modern fixtures. Use it to adjust a benchmark fixture to the candidate ship's age before quoting.
